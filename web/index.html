    About the data

    Outcomes: CAL FIRE damage inspections (DINS) of every residential building. 81 that inspectors couldn't reach are left out.

    Predicted risk: a small gradient-boosting model using year built, neighbor spacing, distance to wildland, fuels and terrain. Nothing recorded after the fire was used. It's a ranking, not a verdict on any one home.
